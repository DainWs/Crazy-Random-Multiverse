# IDEA-001 — Top-down camera

## Problem or opportunity

With the tilted camera, the cards far from the local player shrink with the perspective, so their values are hard to read.

## Proposal

Proposal 01 of the 50 shown to the product owner on 2026-11-21 to make the values of the cards of the board easy to read. The board is seen from straight above, so every point of the table has the same scale.

## Open questions

None.

## Decision

Chosen by the product owner on 2026-11-21, with `IDEA-002` to `IDEA-006`. Specified in `docs/specs/ui/components/camera-view.md` (`VIEW-57`), chosen in the settings screen (`UI-33`), top-down by default, and built in Phase 11.7 (`docs/roadmap/roadmap-archive.md`).
