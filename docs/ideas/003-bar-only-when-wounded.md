# IDEA-003 — Bar only when wounded

## Problem or opportunity

With a bar on every card (`IDEA-002`), the cards that were hit do not stand out from the rest.

## Proposal

Proposal 32 of the 50 of 2026-11-21. A card that has not lost health has no bar, so the cards that were hit stand out on their own. With `IDEA-002`, "wounded" is current health lower than the maximum.

## Open questions

None. It depends on `IDEA-002`, and its rule is still to be written in `docs/specs/ui/` before it is planned.

## Decision

Chosen by the product owner on 2026-11-21.
