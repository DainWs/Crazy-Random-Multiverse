# VIEW-SPECS

Appearance of the interface: layout, animation and sound. Behavior is in `docs/specs/interface.md`. These rules gather what is already known and are expected to change as the game takes shape.

## Board

- **VIEW-1** The local player's zone is at the bottom, with their leader nearest to them. Every other zone is rotated towards its side, with its leader at the edge of the table. The cards of a zone turn with its slots. With the tilted camera (`VIEW-57`) they are drawn inside the four corners of their slot, deformed like it, so they follow its perspective; a card is as wide and as tall as the widest and tallest edges of its slot, and its pieces of equipment are deformed in the same way. With the top-down camera, the default, the slots are not deformed and neither are the cards. Cards in the hand, dragged or in the side panel stay flat. See `docs/guides/phaser4-troubleshooting.md`.
- **VIEW-2** The layout works for 2 to 10 players, with readable cards and hover previews. When few players share the table, the zones are drawn bigger, as much as the room allows: with two players, 1.25 times their base size, keeping a gap of at least 400 board units between the zones that face each other. The scale is rounded down to a multiple of 0.05, so a tiny gain does not change the layout. It never goes under the base size, and with three or more players there is no room, so it stays at 1. The outer edge of the zones stays where it was, and the zones grow towards the center. No two zones overlap and none leaves the table, for any count from 2 to 10.
- **VIEW-3** **[Post-MVP]** Rival hands are shown as card backs, a different one for each type and warrior rarity (`CON-13`).
- The enlargement of a card under the pointer, the side panel and the equipment sliding out (`VIEW-14`), and the line of an own card that can attack (`VIEW-59`), are in `docs/specs/ui/effects/cards-hover.md`.
- The placement of the leaders at the start (`VIEW-12`) is in `docs/specs/ui/effects/game-placement.md`.
- **VIEW-62** The HUD is what Phaser draws over the game while it is played, apart from the cards, and a rule that says "the HUD" means all of this. It is only the look and the place of each of its parts: the animations, the sounds and the effects are not part of the HUD, also those of its parts, so each card can have its own (`FX-22`). Nothing drawn by Vue is part of the HUD, so no skin reaches it (`FX-10`). Decided with the product owner on 2026-11-22.
  - **Part of the HUD:** the buttons (`VIEW-43`, the leave button of a spectator and the button of the game menu), the label of a spectator (`VIEW-28`), the round and the turn (`VIEW-44`), the side panel of a card (`VIEW-14`), the notices of turn, round and elimination (`VIEW-15`, `VIEW-11`), the message of a rejected action (`UI-6`), the confirmation dialog (`UI-15`), the names of the zones, the health bars of the leaders and the mark of who plays next (`VIEW-49` to `VIEW-51`), and the marks of the board of the legend of the help panel (`VIEW-58`: `VIEW-4`, `VIEW-11`, `VIEW-17`, `VIEW-20`, `VIEW-27`, `VIEW-32`).
  - **Not part of the HUD:** the board (table, zones, slots, cards and equipment) and the hand (`VIEW-16`); the animations, the sounds and the effects of every part of the game, those of combat included; the result (`VIEW-23`), which follows the skin of the HUD all the same (`FX-10`); the loading screen (`VIEW-29`); and everything drawn by Vue: the notices of the session and the connection (`UI-9`, `UI-10`, `UI-24`), the main menu and the party, the game menu, the settings screen and the help panel (`UI-33`, `UI-34`, `UI-36`).
- **VIEW-13** The rivals are taken in turn order starting with the one who plays after the local player, and are distributed over the left, top and right sides in that order, clockwise, so each client sees a different table and the turn goes around it. The sides take as many rivals as `VIEW-2` allows, the extra ones going first to the top, then to the left and then to the right, and with a single rival it is on the top. Inside a side the order follows the clockwise direction: from the bottom to the top on the left, from the left to the right on the top, and from the top to the bottom on the right.

- The frame of the leader with the turn (`VIEW-20`), the frame of an own card that can attack (`VIEW-17`) and the pulse of the end turn button (`VIEW-38`) are in `docs/specs/ui/effects/game-pulses.md`.
- **VIEW-19** The wheel zooms the board, and dragging with the right or middle button pans it. Panning never acts on the cards or on the choice of a target (`VIEW-4`), only the primary button does. The wheel does nothing while a card is being dragged. The pan keeps the center of the screen inside the board, so the board never leaves the screen.
- **VIEW-24** The smallest supported window is 1280 by 720, decided with the product owner on 2026-11-21, and the largest checked is 1920 by 1080. At every size from the first to the second, with 2 to 10 players, the HUD, apart from the marks of the board, which are drawn on it, does not overlap the table, the zones or each other, and the hand at rest only overlaps the bottom edge of the table. Below the smallest size the layout is not guaranteed. When the window changes size, the board, the hand and the HUD are placed again, so all of them stay in view and the board stays centered. The camera zoom scales with the window relative to a reference screen of 1920 by 1080, taking the smaller of the two ratios, so at the reference size nothing changes. The board always sits at the same place of the world, so the composition is the same at any size. A pan or a zoom done by the player is lost when the window changes size. A confirmation dialog that is open is closed. Only desktop windows: the responsive layouts are **[Post-MVP]** (`VIEW-10`).
- **VIEW-27** The slots of the local player's zone are tinted blue, fill and border, so their cards are told apart from the rivals', whose slots keep the neutral look. The tint is the resting look of the slot: the marks of `VIEW-32` and `VIEW-17` are drawn over it and the slot goes back to it when a mark ends. An eliminated player's zone (`VIEW-11`) keeps its tint under the veil.
- The buttons of the HUD under the pointer, the border of the confirmation dialog, the label of a spectator (`VIEW-28`), the look and place of the end turn and surrender buttons (`VIEW-43`) and the panel of the round and the turn (`VIEW-44`) are in `docs/specs/ui/components/hud-buttons.md`.
- The loading screen and its change to the board (`VIEW-29`) are in `docs/specs/ui/components/game-loading.md`.
- The name of each zone, the health bar of its leader and the mark of who plays next (`VIEW-49` to `VIEW-51`) are in `docs/specs/ui/components/zones-labels.md`.
- The two cameras of the board, top-down and tilted (`VIEW-57`), are in `docs/specs/ui/components/camera-view.md`.
- The help panel, with the legend of the marks of the board (`VIEW-58`), is in `docs/specs/ui/components/hud-help.md`, and the menu button and the menu that open it (`VIEW-60`) in `docs/specs/ui/components/hud-menu.md`.
- The drag of a card, its marks and the dropped card while the server answers (`VIEW-32`, `VIEW-34`) are in `docs/specs/ui/effects/cards-drag.md`, a card that settles in a slot from an event (`VIEW-35`) in `docs/specs/ui/effects/cards-settle.md`, and the equipment attached to a card (`VIEW-36`) in `docs/specs/ui/effects/cards-equip.md`.
- **VIEW-26** The grid of the table is discreet, so it does not compete with the cards: thin, translucent lines, drawn once as a single path so that their transparency does not pile up.
- **VIEW-45** Only in the accessible mode (`UI-31`), the base game keeps the background as it is. The background of the window around the table is darkened in the game, so the board stands out over it: a black layer at 55% opacity between the background and the board, covering the whole window. The table is translucent, so it is seen a little darker too, but no color of the table, the HUD or the cards changes. It is only in the game, not in the loading screen, the menu or the party. Decided with the product owner on 2026-11-19, and moved to the accessible mode on 2026-11-20.
