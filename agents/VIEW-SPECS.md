# VIEW-SPECS

Appearance of the interface: layout, animation and sound. Behavior is in `specs/INTERFACE.md`. These rules gather what is already known and are expected to change as the game takes shape.

## Board

- **VIEW-1** The local player's zone is at the bottom, with their leader nearest to them. Every other zone is rotated towards its side, with its leader at the edge of the table. The cards of a zone turn with its slots and are centered on the four corners of their slot, not on its projected center. They stay flat rectangles: they do not follow the perspective of the slot (Phase 11 of `ROADMAP.md`).
- **VIEW-2** The layout works for 2 to 10 players, with readable cards and hover previews. When few players share the table, the zones are drawn bigger, as much as the room allows: with two players, 1.25 times their base size, keeping a gap of at least 400 board units between the zones that face each other. The scale is rounded down to a multiple of 0.05, so a tiny gain does not change the layout. It never goes under the base size, and with three or more players there is no room, so it stays at 1. The outer edge of the zones stays where it was, and the zones grow towards the center. No two zones overlap and none leaves the table, for any count from 2 to 10.
- **VIEW-3** **[Post-MVP]** Rival hands are shown as card backs, a different one for each type and warrior rarity (`CON-13`).
- **VIEW-14** Hovering a card enlarges it slightly and shows a panel on the right side of the screen, a quarter of its width and its full height. The panel shows the card at a larger size, its name and its description, and for each piece of equipment its name and the values it adds or subtracts, positive in green and negative in red. The equipment of a combatant is stacked behind it and slides out while hovering. The panel closes when the hovered card disappears, for example when its owner is eliminated (`ELIM-3`). **[Post-MVP]** A card back (`VIEW-3`) shows no panel, because the card is private (`HAND-3`).
- **VIEW-12** When the board is first shown after `GAME_START`, the leaders play an animation of being placed in their slots, one after the other in the order of `zones`, each with its `put` audio once sounds exist (`VIEW-9`). Each leader fades in while it shrinks into its slot, and the whole placement takes about 2 seconds shared among the leaders, with a minimum of 120 ms and a maximum of 500 ms per leader, so with 10 players it takes 200 ms each. The animation of the first cards received (`VIEW-8`) waits for it to end. It is only visual: the leaders are already placed (`SETUP-2`), and events are applied to the state as the queue of `CON-12` delivers them. It does not play when entering a game in progress (`LOAD-6`), also for a client that reloaded the page after confirming and receives `GAME_START` during its new load: it only plays on a client that confirmed `/ready` from the page that shows the board.
- **VIEW-13** The rivals are taken in turn order starting with the one who plays after the local player, and are distributed over the left, top and right sides in that order, clockwise, so each client sees a different table and the turn goes around it. The sides take as many rivals as `VIEW-2` allows, the extra ones going first to the top, then to the left and then to the right, and with a single rival it is on the top. Inside a side the order follows the clockwise direction: from the bottom to the top on the left, from the left to the right on the top, and from the top to the bottom on the right.

- **VIEW-20** The leader of the player with the turn has a glow with a discreet pulse, besides the text of the HUD (`UI-3`). The pulse does not restart when the board is drawn again. It follows the turn of the board being shown, not the latest one received (`CON-12`). If that leader is already dead (`CON-32`), nothing glows.
- **VIEW-19** The wheel zooms the board, and dragging with the right or middle button pans it. Panning never acts on the cards or on the choice of a target (`VIEW-4`), only the primary button does. The wheel does nothing while a card is being dragged. The pan keeps the center of the screen inside the board, so the board never leaves the screen.
- **VIEW-24** When the window changes size, the board, the hand and the HUD are placed again, so all of them stay in view and the board stays centered. The camera zoom scales with the window relative to a reference screen of 1920 by 1080, taking the smaller of the two ratios, so at the reference size nothing changes. The board always sits at the same place of the world, so the composition is the same at any size. A pan or a zoom done by the player is lost when the window changes size. A confirmation dialog that is open is closed. Only desktop windows: the responsive layouts are **[Post-MVP]** (`VIEW-10`).
- **VIEW-27** The slots of the local player's zone are tinted blue, fill and border, so their cards are told apart from the rivals', whose slots keep the neutral look. The tint is the resting look of the slot: the marks of `VIEW-4` and `VIEW-17` are drawn over it and the slot goes back to it when a mark ends. An eliminated player's zone (`VIEW-11`) keeps its tint under the veil.
- **VIEW-26** The grid of the table is discreet, so it does not compete with the cards: thin, translucent lines, drawn once as a single path so that their transparency does not pile up.

## Combat

- **VIEW-4** A card attacks with clicks. Clicking an own card that cannot attack does nothing, and clicking one that can attack selects it: its slot is marked in red, the slots of the cards it can attack in green (`UI-5`), and an arrow goes from it to the pointer, green over a valid target and red elsewhere. A mark is a solid frame drawn over the card, so it stays visible. Over a valid target the arrow stops just before its card, so it does not cover it. Clicking a valid target attacks. Any other click with the primary button, Escape or a change of the game state cancels the selection without a message (`UI-25`), and the marks disappear with it; the other buttons pan (`VIEW-19`). While a card is being dragged, the slot under it is marked in green if the card can go there, and the slot it comes from, if it comes from the board, in red.
- **VIEW-5** A hit shows feedback, such as a shake or a red flash.
- **VIEW-6** Changes in armor and health are animated: the values count down to the new ones, and a floating text over the target shows the armor and health it lost (`CON-21`).
- **VIEW-7** A dead card plays a death animation before disappearing. A leader has its own.
- **VIEW-17** Protected cards and an exposed leader have no mark of their own: what tells them apart is the highlight of the valid targets while attacking (`UI-5`, `PROT-6`). The own cards that can attack now (`attackAvailable`, `CON-15`) do have a mark, while the board being shown is the turn of the local player (`CON-12`): a cyan frame, drawn over the card like those of `VIEW-4`, with a discreet pulse that does not restart when the board is drawn again, as in `VIEW-20`, and that stays still when the system asks for reduced motion. While a card is selected to attack, its own mark is replaced by the red one of `VIEW-4`.
- **VIEW-18** The equipment of a combatant is part of its card (`VIEW-14`): when the card dies (`EQUIP-8`) it plays the death animation together with it and has no animation of its own. The board drawn afterwards has neither.

## Other

- **VIEW-16** The hand has no limit (`HAND-1`), so the more cards it holds, the more they overlap, until each one only shows a minimum strip; from then on the hand grows wider. The hovered card shows whole, because the cards after it slide aside. At rest the hand is mostly hidden at the bottom edge, and it rises when hovered, with an animation. It always folds again when the pointer leaves the hand, also when it leaves the window, and a request made while it is still rising is not dropped. Its cards are drawn at 75% of the size of a card (150 by 225 at the reference screen), so the expanded hand takes about a fifth of the screen height and does not cover the own zone.
- **VIEW-25** The hand is centered horizontally on the screen according to the cards it holds, not according to the space it could take. Its width follows the number of cards, so the cards move to their new slots when one arrives or leaves.
- **VIEW-8** Newly received cards are animated. Cards restored from `GAME_SYNC` are not. **[Post-MVP]** The back of the card a rival receives is animated too (`VIEW-3`).
- **VIEW-9** **[Post-MVP]** Sounds exist for placement, attack, death, turn and round changes, victory and defeat.
- **VIEW-21** **[Post-MVP]** The rarer a card, the more audio variants each of its actions has: for example 1 for placing a Common card and 2 or 3 for a Mythic one.
- **VIEW-22** **[OPEN]** How many variants each rarity has for each action, and how many leaders and equipment have.
- **VIEW-10** **[Post-MVP]** Particles, special effects, reduced motion, color-blind indicators, and responsive and touch layouts.
- **VIEW-11** The zone of an eliminated player is shown empty, under a dark veil and with the label "Eliminado". The cards that leave the board with the elimination (`ELIM-3`) have no animation: they disappear when the board is drawn again. Only a leader killed by an attack plays its death (`VIEW-7`).
- **VIEW-23** The result (`WIN-4`) covers the board with a veil, and its texts, victory or defeat, the winner and the countdown, sit on a panel, so the slots of the board do not show through them.
- **VIEW-15** A brief notice announces each turn change, with "Tu turno" or the name of the player whose turn starts, and each new round, with "Ronda N". A notice is a text on a dark panel, centered horizontally and a fifth of the screen height below its top edge, so it does not hide the leader of the rival at the top. It fades in for 0.2 s, stays 1.1 s and fades out for 0.2 s. When the round and the turn change together, the round is announced first and then the turn. It is one more animation of the queue of `CON-12`, so the first turn and round of a game are announced too, and entering a game in progress (`LOAD-6`) announces nothing.
