# IDEA-004 — Numbers on the card

## Problem or opportunity

The damage and the armor of a card of the board have to be read without floating information.

## Proposal

The face of a card of the board shows its damage, large, and its armor; the health is the bar of `IDEA-002`. The exact health is read in the side panel (`VIEW-14`) and with the key of `IDEA-005`.
