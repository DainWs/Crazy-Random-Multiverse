# IDEA-002 — Health bar on the card

## Problem or opportunity

The values of the cards of the board are hard to read, and the values drawn outside the cards were removed on 2026-11-21. The health has to be read without floating information.

## Proposal

Proposal 31 of the 50 of 2026-11-21. One bar along the bottom of every card of the board, as wide as the card, filled in green with the current health over the maximum (`CON-27`); what was lost is drawn dark. The bar has no armor part: the contract has no maximum armor, and the product owner chose not to add it.

## Open questions

None. Its rule is still to be written in `docs/specs/ui/` before it is planned.

## Decision

Chosen by the product owner on 2026-11-21.
