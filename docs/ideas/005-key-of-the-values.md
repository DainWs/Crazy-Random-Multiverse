# IDEA-005 — Key of the values

## Problem or opportunity

The exact values of every card of the board cannot be read at once without hovering each card.

## Proposal

Proposal 42 of the 50 of 2026-11-21. While the player holds Alt down, every card of the board shows only its values: dark face, large damage and health, smaller armor. When it is released, the cards go back to their face.

Known risk: on Windows, releasing Alt can take the focus to the menu of the browser, and the page cannot always prevent it.

## Open questions

None. Its rule is still to be written in `docs/specs/ui/` before it is planned.

## Decision

Chosen by the product owner on 2026-11-21.
